How Notely writes notes from a capture

When you capture a screen or regenerate key points, Notely sends the screenshot and the text read from it (and what you said, if you used Start speaking) to Google's Gemini API for processing. Google's terms and privacy policy apply. A screenshot shows everything on your screen at that moment, which can include private or sensitive information such as messages, emails, account details or passwords, so check your screen before capturing. No screenshot or text is sent until you agree, and you can turn this off at any time by choosing Rules only or Local AI.
